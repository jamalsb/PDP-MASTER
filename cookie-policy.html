If analytics, Google AdSense, or other advertising services are enabled, those providers may use cookies, local storage, web beacons, or similar technologies to measure usage, prevent fraud, personalize or deliver ads, and report performance, subject to applicable settings and laws. This policy must be updated to identify the services actually enabled before they are activated.

Managing cookies

You can review, block, or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect third-party features. Where consent is legally required, the website should request it before setting non-essential cookies.
