Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Master handles information when you visit and use this website.

Files you select

PDF processing is designed to take place locally in your browser. Files you select for the PDF tools are not intentionally uploaded to a PDF Master document-processing server, and PDF Master does not currently offer accounts or cloud file storage. Your browser may keep temporary data in memory while a task is running.

Information collected by hosting and service providers

When you visit a website, its hosting provider and infrastructure may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs. PDF Master currently uses third-party content delivery networks to load software libraries. Those providers may receive standard connection information when your browser requests those resources. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

PDF Master does not currently state that it operates its own analytics or advertising cookies. If analytics, advertising services such as Google AdSense, or other cookie-based features are added, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data use. Third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies and settings.

How information is used

Technical information may be used by hosting and infrastructure providers to deliver the website, maintain security, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse. We do not sell personal information.

Children's privacy

This website is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through public contact channels.

Your choices and questions

You can control cookies through your browser settings. For privacy questions, use the contact route on our Contact Us page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website features, providers, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.
